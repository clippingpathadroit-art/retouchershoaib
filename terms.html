1. Scope of Services & Free Trial

Retoucher Shoaib provides commercial photo post-production across jewelry, fashion, editorial beauty, e-commerce packshots, still life, and automotive imagery.

New commercial clients may submit 1 to 2 sample images for a complimentary trial prior to confirming paid production batches. Trial deliveries reflect the exact non-destructive craft, color calibration, and layered standards applied to full campaigns.

2. Turnaround, Quality Control & Revisions

  • Standard Delivery: Single samples and moderate batches are typically returned within 12 to 24 hours. High-volume campaign schedules are agreed upon before production begins.
  • Multi-Pass Inspection: Every asset goes through full-zoom manual QC prior to delivery.
  • Revisions: Revisions within the agreed creative brief are included promptly at no extra charge. Material scope changes requested after final delivery are quoted separately prior to work.

3. Copyright & Client Intellectual Property

Clients retain 100% ownership and full worldwide copyright of all original photography, RAW files, and final delivered assets (TIFF, PSD, JPEG, or WebP). Retoucher Shoaib claims no copyright over your brand photography and does not resell or license client imagery.

4. Confidentiality, Embargoes & Data Protection

All client uploads and campaign briefs are treated as strictly confidential. Unreleased product launches and embargoed editorial sets are never shared publicly, used in marketing showcases without permission, or supplied to AI training datasets. Full data handling details are documented in our Privacy Policy.

5. Quotes, Invoicing & Payments

Pricing is quoted per project scope, retouching complexity, and batch volume prior to commencement. Invoices issued inside the Client Portal remain due according to agreed studio payment terms.

6. Client Portal & Acceptable Use

Access to retouchershoaib.com/portal is granted for submitting legitimate retouching briefs, reviewing deliveries, and managing invoices. You agree not to submit unlawful material, attempt unauthorized access to other client workspaces, or interfere with portal availability.

7. Contact Information

For questions regarding these terms, custom studio SLAs, or signed NDAs, contact retouchershoaib@gmail.com or visit retouchershoaib.com/contact.
