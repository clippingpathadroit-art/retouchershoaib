Jewelry and watches sell on tiny details

Online buyers cannot rotate a solitaire under shop light or hold a timepiece on their wrist. Instead, they pinch to zoom straight into the prongs, pavé seatings, dial typography and clasp engravings. A yellow tint on white gold, hazy facets under studio dust, or glare masking the minute track quietly stalls the sale—or triggers a costly return.

We work with fine jewelers, bespoke fine jewellery ateliers, bridal houses, watch dealers and commercial packshot photographers across the US, UK, Europe and Switzerland. Every frame is cleaned manually in 16-bit Photoshop and inspected at full 200% zoom.

Our Honesty Rule — Zero False Claims

Retouching should show a piece at its absolute best, never misrepresent what the customer receives. We never falsify a diamond’s clarity grade, push a gemstone into an artificial color grade, shift a 14K alloy to impersonate another metal, or paint out genuine scratches and wear on pre-owned watch listings. When a request risks misleading buyers, we flag it upfront and recommend an honest studio fix.

02 Metal Color Correction

White gold color fix & true yellow, rose & platinum tones

Fix why white gold looks yellow in photos · Build multi-metal SKU sets

Mixed studio strobes, white bounce paper and warm tungsten fill turn crisp rhodium-plated white gold faintly yellow—while push-button global saturation makes 14K rose gold look copper and 18K yellow gold look brassy. We isolate metal channels independently from stones to hold exact alloy tone across every product card.

Precision metal tone standards

  • White gold & platinum: keep white gold bright cool-rhodium and retain platinum’s richer subtle grey density
  • Yellow gold: warm, luminous karat balance without turning orange or greenish-brass
  • Rose gold: soft genuine copper-blush alloy tone without clipping into pink or orange
  • Sterling silver: clean bright silver contrast with dark tarnish & oxidation spots cleared

How to shoot jewelry and watches for faster, sharper retouching

Cleaner camera captures mean faster delivery and lower cost per SKU. Follow these 3 visual steps and 8-point set checklist whether shooting brand packshots or Chrono24 dealer inventory:

Shot 1 · Tent & Flags Lock tripod height inside a diffused tent with black negative-fill cards framing crisp metal edges.
Shot 2 · Focus Stack Slices Capture 3 to 8 overlapping focal planes (front claws to back shank) for edge-to-edge macro sharpness.
Shot 3 · Dial & 18% Reference Add an 18% grey card shot (or daylight phone snap) plus a glare-free dial pass with hands at 10:10.

Frequently asked questions

Clear answers before you send your first diamond solitaire, gemstone strand or watch collection.

What is jewelry retouching?

Jewelry and fine jewellery photo retouching is the careful manual post-production of ring, necklace, earring, bracelet, gemstone, and watch photos to remove dust, glue wax and polish marks, sculpt clean studio metal reflections, correct yellow casts, refine prongs, and bring out each gemstone’s natural fire—so the image matches how the piece looks in hand.

How much does jewelry and watch retouching cost?

Simple hand-drawn cutouts and pure white (#FFFFFF) isolation on clean chains or plain bands start from $0.50 per image. Multi-stone engagement rings, prong cleanup, focus stacks, multi-metal variants and luxury watch crystal glare work are quoted custom based on complexity and monthly batch volume (with volume tiers for 50 to 500+ images). Send 1–2 sample frames for a firm quote and free proof.

Can you make white gold look like white gold, not yellow?

Yes. Warm studio bounces often shift rhodium-plated white gold yellow in camera RAW captures. Using your physical piece or grey card reference, we restore neutral bright white gold while preserving platinum’s authentic cool-grey character.

Can you change gold to rose gold or silver in a photo?

Yes, provided the exact piece is manufactured in those alloys. Send one clean shot plus metal references—we isolate shanks and prongs away from diamonds so each SKU colorway matches what the customer unboxes.

Will you make my diamonds look better than they are?

No. Under our Honesty Rule, we bring out the brilliance and contrast a stone genuinely has, while clearing surface lint. We never fake clarity grades, invent gemstone colors, or paint over permanent inclusions in a way that misleads buyers.

How do you handle glare on watch crystals?

We remove distracting softbox blocks and purple/green anti-reflective coating haze covering hands, indices or dial text while leaving a clean, subtle glass edge reflection so the sapphire crystal still looks tactile. Where domed crystals warp dial typography slightly, we correct geometry carefully.

Do you hide scratches on pre-owned watches?

No. Resale collectors need honest condition transparency. We clean dust, fingerprints and murky room glare cleanly, while keeping real case dings, bezel wear and bracelet scratches untouched.

Can you work from focus-stacked files?

Yes. Upload bracketed RAW shots or a combined stack file—we hand-clean haloes and align seams between prongs, tables and rear bands at 200% zoom until every millimeter is razor-sharp.

Do you use AI filters or automated tools?

Never. Automated AI tools smudge hallmarks, create plastic gradients and chop holes through transparent diamonds. Every order is completed manually in 16-bit Photoshop with non-destructive layers and hand-drawn Pen Tool paths.

How fast can you deliver?

Standard orders return in 12 to 24 hours, timed overnight for US, UK, Swiss and European teams. Large catalog drops complete in 24 to 48 hours with Style-Lock QA, and launch rush delivery is available anytime.
