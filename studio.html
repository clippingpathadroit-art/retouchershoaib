The founder’s journey

My name is Shoaib. Over the last decade I’ve worked with commercial photographers, fashion designers, e-commerce brands and jewelry studios across North America, the UK, Germany and France.

As international campaigns grew, I built a hand-picked team of 10 to 15 senior retouchers. We aren’t a bulk-outsourcing mill. We work as a boutique collective, and every image goes through multi-pass manual quality control.
