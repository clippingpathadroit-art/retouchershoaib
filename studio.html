About the Studio

We are Retoucher Shoaib®

A specialized commercial post-production studio built around pixel precision, color integrity, and non-destructive craft. Directed by Shoaib with a collective of 10–15 elite retouchers.

The Founder's Journey

10+ Years of Dedication to High-End Post-Production.

My name is Shoaib. Over the last decade, I have collaborated with renowned commercial photographers, fashion designers, e-commerce empires, and jewelry studios across North America, the UK, Germany, and France.

As the volume of international campaigns grew, I formed a hand-picked, specialized studio team of 10 to 15 senior retouchers. Rather than running an impersonal bulk-outsourcing mill, our studio operates as a boutique collective: every image undergoes multi-pass manual quality control, non-destructive dodge & burn, and 100% zoom inspection.
