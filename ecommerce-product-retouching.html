Frequently asked questions

Everything online brands, photographers and catalog managers ask before sending their first e-commerce batch.

How much does e-commerce product photo retouching cost?

Simple hand-drawn Pen Tool clipping paths and pure white (#FFFFFF) packshot cutouts start from $0.50 per image. Detailed eyewear glare removal, strap compositing, footwear pair alignment, cosmetics glass cleanup and multi-colorway SKU recoloring are quoted custom depending on complexity and monthly volume. Send 1–2 samples for an exact quote within hours.

Can you remove glare from glasses without making the lenses look fake?

Yes. Removing 100% of reflection makes lenses look empty like cardboard cutouts. We clear distracting softbox squares, camera silhouettes and dust while crafting a subtle, true-to-life anti-reflective glass sheen gradient.

Can you make a pair of shoes symmetrical without losing leather grain or suede nap?

Yes. We balance left and right shoe height, heel angle and toe-box creases cleanly by hand while preserving 100% of natural pebble grain, suede texture, knit mesh and outsole stitching.

Can you recolor one product photo into multiple SKU colorways?

Yes. Send one photographed master sample alongside fabric swatches or Pantone/Hex codes. We apply targeted panel masking so body colorways update cleanly while white pockets, stitch threads, logos and metal hardware remain untouched.

Do you use automated AI filters or bulk cutout presets?

Never. Every single image is retouched manually in 16-bit Photoshop using hand-drawn Pen Tool paths, frequency separation and manual dodge and burn—so zero plastic skin, zero blurred grain and zero jagged auto-masking halos enter your storefront.

How fast can you deliver large seasonal product drops?

Standard orders return within 12 to 24 hours. High-volume seasonal drops of 150+ frames typically complete in 24 to 48 hours using our Style-Lock reference check, with overnight shifts matched to US, UK and European shoot schedules.

What file formats do I send, and what will I receive back?

Upload camera RAW files (CR3, ARW, NEF, DNG) or TIFF, PSD, JPG and PNG folders through your private Cloudflare R2 Client Portal (/portal) or Drive/Dropbox/WeTransfer. We return layered PSDs with Pen Paths, flattened TIFFs or marketplace-ready JPG/WebP files in sRGB, Adobe RGB or print CMYK.

Can you follow strict Amazon, Shopify, Etsy or wholesale portal specs?

Yes. Send your exact aspect ratios, fill percentages, canvas dimensions (#FFFFFF pure white or custom gray), file naming rules and folder taxonomy—every image exports ready for immediate upload.

Are pre-launch collections protected under NDA?

Yes. All files remain strictly private for your production only, served via encrypted client delivery, and we sign mutual NDAs before you upload pre-release samples.
