Behind the Craft: How We Deliver High-Volume Perfection.

A calibrated 3-stage post-production pipeline built over 10 years to eliminate agency bottlenecks while safeguarding pixel-perfect luxury quality.

STAGE 01
< 2 Hours

RAW Ingestion & Hardware Color Calibration

Incoming high-res RAW capture files (Hasselblad IIQ, Phase One, Canon CR3, Sony ARW) ingested directly into 16-bit ProPhoto RGB. Calibrated on EIZO ColorEdge CG2700X hardware monitors with strict Pantone color-syncing against physical textile swatches (ΔE < 1.0).

ProPhoto RGB 16-Bit EIZO CG2700X ΔE < 1.0 Sync
STAGE 02
12–18 Hours

Multi-Pass Dodge & Burn Defect Sculpting

Two senior artists perform dual-pass non-destructive dodging and burning: macro luminosity harmonization followed by microscopic defect markup. Stray hairs, sensor dust, metal scratches, and fabric tension creases meticulously resolved without blurring filters.

Non-Destructive D&B Zero Gaussian Blur Anatomical Pore Masking
STAGE 03
< 4 Hours

Studio Director QA & Certified Prepress Delivery

Studio Director Shoaib personally reviews every master plate at 100% and 200% zoom. Delivered as layered PSDs with non-destructive adjustment masks, CMYK prepress certifications (ISO Coated v2 / GRACoL 2013), and cloud delivery via secure high-speed links.

Layered Master PSDs CMYK Prepress Cert 100% NDA Protection
