Questions we get a lot

Can’t find yours? Message us and we’ll answer today.

How does the free trial work?

Send one or two images with notes. We retouch them for free and return them within 24 hours, so you can judge the quality before committing to anything.

How fast can you deliver?

Most orders come back in 12 to 24 hours. Larger batches take up to 48 hours, and rush delivery can be arranged for urgent shoots.

Will skin and metal still look natural?

Yes. We avoid heavy blur and keep real texture, like pores on skin and grain in metal, so the result looks polished but believable.

Do you use automated AI filters or bulk templates?

No. Every image is retouched manually by senior artists in 16-bit Photoshop using non-destructive layers, pen paths, and dodge and burn—never one-click AI smoothing.

Can you match our existing style?

Send a few approved reference images or a short style note. We match your look and keep it consistent across the whole batch.

Are my files kept private?

Your files are used only for your order. We can sign an NDA on request for unreleased products.

Do you work across US and European time zones?

Yes. With rotating studio shifts, photographers and brands in New York, London, Milan, Paris and Scandinavia upload at the end of their shoot day and receive finished proofs in 12 to 24 hours.

What if I need changes?

Tell us what to adjust and we revise it. Revisions are part of the order, not an extra charge.
