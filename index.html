What’s included

Every order is checked against the same list, so results stay consistent from the first image to the last.

Jewelry and watches

For rings, necklaces, earrings and watches that have to sparkle without looking fake.

  • Dust, scratch and fingerprint removal
  • Gemstone brightness and facet clarity
  • Focus stacking and reflection cleanup
  • Metal color matched to the real piece

Fashion and apparel

For lookbooks, catalogs and online stores with large batches.

  • Wrinkle and lint removal
  • Fabric shape and symmetry correction
  • Color matching to swatches
  • Ghost mannequin and neck joins

Editorial beauty

Skin that looks like skin, for magazines and campaigns.

  • Dodge and burn, no heavy blur
  • Blemish and flyaway hair cleanup
  • Eye, lip and teeth refinement
  • Color grading to your reference

Product packshots

For stores that need a clean, repeatable look at volume.

  • Hand-drawn pen-tool clipping paths
  • Custom shadows and reflections
  • Pure white or colored backgrounds
  • Marketplace-ready exports

Portraits

For headshots, team pages and talent profiles.

  • Even light and skin tone
  • Background cleanup
  • Clothing and collar fixes
  • One consistent look across a team

Questions we get a lot

Can’t find yours? Message us and we’ll answer today.

How does the free trial work?

Send one or two images with notes. We retouch them for free and return them within 24 hours, so you can judge the quality before committing to anything.

How fast can you deliver?

Most orders come back in 12 to 24 hours. Larger batches take up to 48 hours, and rush delivery can be arranged for urgent shoots.

Will skin and metal still look natural?

Yes. We avoid heavy blur and keep real texture, like pores on skin and grain in metal, so the result looks polished but believable.

Can you match our existing style?

Send a few approved reference images or a short style note. We match your look and keep it consistent across the whole batch.

Are my files kept private?

Your files are used only for your order. We can sign an NDA on request for unreleased products.

What if I need changes?

Tell us what to adjust and we revise it. Revisions are part of the order, not an extra charge.
