01 Invisible Mannequin · 3D Neck Joint

Ghost mannequin photo editing: the invisible look, done right

Ghost mannequin photography shows a garment holding its worn 3D fit with no stand visible. Because exterior frames and inside lining are blended by hand, any automated shortcut looks flat or pasted on.

What goes wrong

  • Visible necks, arms, pins, clamps or tape
  • Flat, hollow collar openings lacking 3D depth
  • Crooked plackets and uneven sleeve hems
  • Creases, lint, missing labels or lining shade shifts

What we do

  • Hand-drawn Pen Tool cutouts along real seams
  • 3D inside-collar neck joins with natural depth
  • Balanced shoulder, sleeve, cuff & hem drape
  • Crease cleanup and soft grounded floor shadows
Inside labels & care tags

We keep, clean, straighten or composite woven neck labels from your close-up frame—matching fabric perspective, weave grain and natural interior shadow.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to every question fashion directors, e-commerce managers and photographers ask before sending a first garment test.

What is ghost mannequin photo editing?

It’s the manual compositing process of combining multiple photographs of a garment—usually a styled exterior shot on a mannequin bust plus a separate shot of the inside back collar or lining—and removing the mannequin completely. The final image displays natural 3D fit, depth and your inside label without any model or support visible.

How much does ghost mannequin editing cost?

Simple flat-lay garment cutouts and single-frame isolation start from $0.50 per image. Multi-shot 3D ghost mannequin neck joins depend on garment complexity and number of views—basic knit tops cost less than lined structured blazers or sheer lace dresses, with tiered volume discounts for 50 to 500+ frame seasonal drops. Send 1–2 sample files and we’ll reply with an exact firm quote.

What shots do you need for a clean neck join?

We need one main front frame photographed cleanly on the mannequin, paired with a second shot capturing the inside back collar and lining taken from the same tripod position. If you want the neck or care label highlighted clearly, add a sharp frame of it—see our 8-step shooting checklist above.

Can you add or replace the inside label?

Yes. Upload a separate label photograph or close-up reference and we blend it seamlessly inside the reconstructed collar—matching fabric warp, woven texture, perspective curve and realistic neck shadow.

Can you edit ghost mannequin photos for sheer or light fabrics?

Yes. Instead of making translucent chiffon, organza, mesh or fine lace look opaque and plastic, we extract edges manually and retain true optical translucency against white or soft studio backgrounds.

Do you use AI filters or automated tools?

No. Every garment and model image is retouched manually in 16-bit Photoshop using non-destructive layers, hand-drawn Pen Tool clipping paths and manual dodge and burn. We never run automated AI smoothing or bulk cutout presets that blur delicate knits or distort seams.

Will you change a model’s body or face?

Only if you specifically request it in your markup notes. By default we preserve natural human features and proportions while focusing strictly on clean skin texture, flyaways, clothing folds and balanced batch lighting.

How fast can you deliver?

Standard orders deliver within 12 to 24 hours. Large seasonal batches of 150+ frames typically complete in 24 to 48 hours using Style-Lock QA, and urgent launch rush scheduling can be arranged anytime.

Can you match colors exactly to my product?

Yes. Send physical fabric swatch photos, Pantone/Hex targets or approved campaign references alongside the shoot—especially on tough blacks, dark navies and bright reds. We recover crushed shadow knit detail and supply calibrated web sRGB alongside Adobe RGB or print CMYK files.

Are my files kept private?

Strictly. Your raw captures and unreleased styles are used exclusively for your production order inside encrypted Cloudflare R2 transfers, and we sign mutual NDAs before upload whenever needed.
